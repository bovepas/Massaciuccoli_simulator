DELTA-SHAP INTERPRETATION:
Delta-SHAP for a feature is calculated as its SHAP value in the scenario minus its SHAP value in the baseline.
A positive Delta-SHAP contributes positively to the scenario-minus-baseline risk difference. A negative Delta-SHAP contributes negatively.
The sign describes the change in the model attribution, not whether the environmental variable itself increased or decreased.
Delta-SHAP values describe model attribution changes; they do not establish ecological causation.

DIRECTLY MODIFIED FEATURES:
This list identifies features explicitly modified in the requested scenario. Their contributions are shown separately and are not necessarily the largest contributors to the overall risk-score difference.

OTHER FEATURE CONTRIBUTIONS:
These are attribution changes for other model features. Their presence does not mean that the corresponding environmental variables were directly modified or that they physically changed during the simulation.

The overall risk-score difference reflects the combined contributions. Positive and negative contributions can offset one another, so the total can decrease even when some individual contributions are positive.
Do not present these contributions as ecological causes unless independent supporting evidence establishes the relevant mechanism.
