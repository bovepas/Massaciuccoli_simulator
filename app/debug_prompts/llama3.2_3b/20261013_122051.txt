=========================================================
TARGET
=========================================================

Extract the biological target of the question.

The target can be:

- a specific species, identified by its common name or scientific name:
  "luccio", "Esox lucius", "Aeshna mixta", "Species X"
- a broad biological category:
  "pesci", "uccelli", "rettili", "anfibi", "mammiferi",
  "crostacei", "insetti", "molluschi"

Set:

target_type = "species"
when the target refers to a specific species, whether identified by
its common name, scientific name, or a placeholder such as "Species X".

target_type = "category"
when the target refers to a broad biological group rather than
a specific species.

IMPORTANT CLASSIFICATION RULES:

- A named species is ALWAYS "species", even if its common name is
  unfamiliar or its scientific name is not provided.
- The word "species" or "specie" does NOT mean that the target is
  a biological category.
- Words describing the question, such as "habitat", "distribution",
  "suitability" or "environmental conditions", do NOT determine
  the target_type.
- For example:
  "Come è distribuita la specie luccio sul territorio del lago?"
  -> target = "luccio", target_type = "species"

  "Come è distribuita la specie Esox lucius sul territorio del lago?"
  -> target = "Esox lucius", target_type = "species"

  "Quali condizioni ambientali sono più adatte ai pesci?"
  -> target = "pesci", target_type = "category"

  "How is the distribution of birds across the lake?"
  -> target = "birds", target_type = "category"

- Never classify a specific species as a category merely because
  its name is unfamiliar or because the question uses the word
  "species".
- Do not infer a category from the species name.
- Do not replace a common name with a scientific name, or vice versa.
- Keep the target exactly as expressed by the user, apart from
  removing surrounding words that are not part of the target.

The target is mandatory.
Never omit it.
Never invent it.

If the question does not contain an identifiable biological target,
do not guess one.

=========================================================
HYPOTHETICAL CHANGE
=========================================================

For hypothetical_effect, extract:

variable
variation
variation_type

The variable is the environmental variable being changed.

Examples:
- temperature
- precipitation
- evapotranspiration
- tree cover
- biodiversity

Examples of variation:

"+2°C" -> variation = "+2°C"
"-20%" -> variation = "-20%"
"increased evapotranspiration" -> variation = "increase"
"reduced tree cover" -> variation = "decrease"

variation_type must be one of:

"absolute"
"percentage"
"relative"
"qualitative"

Use:

absolute = explicit absolute numerical change
percentage = explicit percentage change
relative = multiplicative change such as "doubles" or "halves"
qualitative = non-numerical change such as "increases" or "decreases"

Do NOT calculate or transform the requested change.
