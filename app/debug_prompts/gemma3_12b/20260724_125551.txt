focused on specific environmental components or restricted portions of

• hydrological dynamics and water quality;

• biodiversity conservation;

Despite the richness of these thematic investigations, no previous
study has provided a comprehensive, spatially explicit ecosystem-risk
assessment integrating anthropogenic pressures, climatic drivers,
ecological fragility and biodiversity indicators across the entire
Massaciuccoli Lake basin.

Ecosystem Risk Assessment (ERA) aims to identify, quantify and map the
probability that ecological systems experience degradation due to
natural or anthropogenic stressors.

Risk is generally interpreted as the combination of ecosystem exposure,
ecological vulnerability and the intensity of environmental stressors.

Spatial ecosystem-risk assessment supports environmental monitoring,
conservation planning and prioritisation of management actions by
identifying areas characterised by different ecological risk levels.
