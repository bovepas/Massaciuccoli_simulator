The collection is organised into five temporal reference frames:
- Remote past: 1950–1980; representative year 1950. - Near past: 1981–2015; representative year 2000. - Present: 2016–2024; representative year 2024. - Near future: 2025–2050; representative year 2050. - Far future: 2051–2100; representative year 2100. Near-future and far-future projections are distinguished, where available, by Representative Concentration Pathways (RCP):
- RCP 2.6: low greenhouse-gas concentration scenario. - RCP 4.5: medium greenhouse-gas concentration scenario. - RCP 8.5: high greenhouse-gas concentration scenario. Not every variable is available for every time frame or RCP scenario. Availability must be checked in the catalogue metadata rather than assumed. SPATIAL REFERENCE AND HARMONISATION
===================================

The geodata are georeferenced in WGS84 / EPSG:4326 and clipped to the boundaries of the Massaciuccoli Lake basin. Raster data are resampled to a common spatial resolution of 0.0005 degrees, approximately 50 metres. This resolution was selected as a suitable compromise for regional-scale ecological models and monitoring analyses. The collection is packaged in a QGIS project, including the datasets, legends and visualisation styles. A metadata table in Excel format describes the geodata. A PDF cartographic atlas provides a visual representation of the layers and their metadata. The standardised dataset names encode:
1. Dataset identifier. 2. Standardised geodata/variable name. 3. Reference time frame. 4. RCP scenario, where applicable. For example, `063_temperatures_avg_2100ff_rcp85` identifies dataset 063, average temperature, far-future time frame (2100), RCP 8.5. VARIABLES RELEVANT TO THE DIGITAL TWIN AND BENCHMARK RETRIEVAL
