SCENARIO MODIFICATIONS:
The scenario modifications section lists the feature values actually applied to the model input.
These modifications must not be confused with model contributions.

PRIMARY DELTA-SHAP CONTRIBUTIONS:
These are model attribution changes associated with features identified as modified in the scenario.
They are not necessarily the largest contributors to the overall risk-score difference.

SECONDARY DELTA-SHAP CONTRIBUTIONS:
These are attribution changes for other model features.
Their presence does not mean that the corresponding environmental variables were directly modified or that they physically changed during the simulation.

DELTA-SHAP INTERPRETATION:
Delta-SHAP for a feature is calculated as its SHAP value in the scenario minus its SHAP value in the baseline.
A positive Delta-SHAP contributes positively to the scenario-minus-baseline risk difference. A negative Delta-SHAP contributes negatively.
The sign describes the change in model attribution, not whether the environmental variable itself increased or decreased.
Delta-SHAP values describe model attribution changes; they do not establish ecological causation.

The overall risk-score difference reflects the combined contributions. Positive and negative contributions can offset one another.
Do not present these contributions as ecological causes unless independent supporting evidence establishes the relevant mechanism.
