TASK

The user asks the Massaciuccoli Digital Twin to evaluate how a
hypothetical environmental scenario differs from the current
ecosystem conditions.

The current ecosystem state is used as the baseline reference.

The Digital Twin has already simulated the requested environmental
scenario and compared it with the baseline ecosystem.

Your task is not to perform the assessment again.

Your task is to explain the ecological meaning of the reported
assessment using only the information provided by the Digital Twin.

To prepare your answer, the Digital Twin provides three complementary
sources of information.

1. DIGITAL TWIN ASSESSMENT REPORT

This report contains the simulated environmental scenario,
the predicted ecosystem response,
the primary model drivers,
and the secondary ecosystem associations.

Treat this report as the authoritative description of the
simulation results.

2. ECOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION NOTES

These notes explain the ecological role of the environmental
variables reported by the Digital Twin.

They help interpret the reported drivers and ecosystem
associations but do not modify the simulation results.

3. RETRIEVED SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE

These excerpts were retrieved from the curated scientific
knowledge base describing the Massaciuccoli Lake ecosystem.

Use the retrieved scientific evidence only to explain
the ecological relevance of variables already reported
by the Digital Twin.

Do not extend the simulated scenario by introducing
additional ecological consequences that are not
explicitly reported in the Assessment Report.

Use the retrieved scientific evidence only to explain
the ecological meaning of the variables already
identified by the Digital Twin.

The retrieved evidence must support the reported
assessment.

It must not be used to predict additional ecological
effects.

When the retrieved evidence supports the reported model
drivers or ecosystem associations, integrate that evidence
naturally into your explanation.

Do not introduce ecological mechanisms that are unsupported
by the retrieved scientific evidence.

Remember that secondary ecosystem associations describe
variables statistically associated with the simulated
environmental scenario.

They do not necessarily indicate variables that increased,
decreased or otherwise changed during the simulation.

Focus your explanation on the ecological implications of the
comparison between the simulated scenario and the baseline
ecosystem rather than repeating numerical values.

Produce a concise scientific explanation that directly answers the user's original question.
