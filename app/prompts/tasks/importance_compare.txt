TASK

The user asks the Massaciuccoli Digital Twin to compare
the relative importance of two environmental variables
with respect to predicted ecosystem risk.

The Digital Twin has already computed the comparison.

Your task is not to compare the variables again.

Your task is to explain the reported comparison using
the scientific information provided below.

To prepare your answer, the Digital Twin provides three
complementary sources of information.

1. DIGITAL TWIN IMPORTANCE COMPARISON REPORT

This report identifies:

- the compared environmental variables;
- their importance scores;
- the variable showing the stronger statistical
  association with predicted ecosystem risk.

Treat the Importance Comparison Report as the
authoritative description of the Digital Twin analysis.

Do not modify the reported comparison.

Do not determine a different ranking.

Do not compare the scores beyond what is already
reported by the Digital Twin.

2. ECOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION NOTES

The reported importance scores describe the relative
statistical sensitivity of the compared variables
within the current Digital Twin prediction.

A higher importance score indicates a stronger
statistical association with predicted ecosystem risk.

The reported comparison does not represent observed
environmental changes and should not be interpreted
as direct ecological causality.

3. RETRIEVED SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE

These excerpts were retrieved from the curated
scientific knowledge base describing the
Massaciuccoli Lake ecosystem.

Use the retrieved scientific evidence only to explain
the ecological relevance of the reported variables.

Focus primarily on the ecological interpretation of
the higher-ranked variable.

The second variable may be mentioned only to provide
ecological context.

Do not infer ecological mechanisms unless they are
supported by both the Digital Twin outputs and the
retrieved scientific evidence.

Do not introduce additional environmental variables,
ecological processes, management actions,
conservation recommendations or unsupported
conclusions.

Write one concise scientific explanation that directly
answers the user's question.
