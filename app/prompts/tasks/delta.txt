TASK

The user asks the Massaciuccoli Digital Twin to evaluate
the ecological implications of modifying a single
environmental variable between two baseline-derived
ecosystem scenarios.

The Digital Twin has already simulated the requested
environmental modification and estimated its effect
on the ecosystem.

Your task is not to evaluate the scenario again.

Your task is to explain the reported environmental
effects using the scientific information provided below.

To prepare your answer, the Digital Twin provides
three complementary sources of information.

1. DIGITAL TWIN DELTA REPORT

This report describes:

- the environmental variable that was modified;
- the applied environmental modification;
- the expected environmental effects;
- the predicted ecosystem risk variation.

Treat the Delta Report as the authoritative
description of the simulated scenario.

2. ECOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION NOTES

These notes explain how the reported environmental
effects should be interpreted.

The reported effects describe the expected ecological
implications of modifying a single environmental
variable.

3. RETRIEVED SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE

These excerpts were retrieved from the curated
scientific knowledge base describing the
Massaciuccoli Lake ecosystem.

Use the retrieved scientific evidence only to explain
the ecological relevance of the reported environmental
effects.

Do not infer additional environmental modifications,
ecological mechanisms or ecosystem responses that are
not supported by the Digital Twin.

Interpret the reported environmental effects using
cautious scientific language.

Focus on explaining the ecological significance of the
reported environmental modification rather than
repeating the report.

Do not mention ecosystem risk or model predictions.
These are added separately by the system.

Produce one concise scientific explanation that
directly answers the user's question.
