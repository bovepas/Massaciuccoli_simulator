Massaciuccoli Digital Twin — FAIR Dataset Catalogue

Version: 1.0 — initial consolidated inventory
Date: 4 November 2026
Scope: documentary inventory of datasets and data resources cited or
used by the reviewed sources.
Excluded from this phase: paper1, paper2, and paper3, as requested.

1. Interpretation and status rules

This catalogue distinguishes a publication from the data associated with
it. An article DOI does not automatically identify a dataset. A
catalogue record or a declared download does not prove that the actual
files have been downloaded, inspected, or validated.

Status labels used here:

-   Verified FAIR resource: the resource has been explicitly verified as
    FAIR for this project, or the publication provides a clearly
    identified open repository and the resource identity/access route is
    established. File-level technical validation may still be recorded
    separately.
-   Partially verified / access declared: an access route is stated by
    the source, but the actual files, metadata, formats, licence, or
    completeness have not all been independently checked.
-   Catalogue identified / operational verification pending: a catalogue
    entry or distribution is identified, but the actual data package and
    its properties still require inspection.
-   Not verified: a source mentions a database or dataset, but its
    identity, access, or reuse conditions have not been established.
-   Published map/document only: a cartographic or documentary output is
    available, but editable source data are not confirmed.

FAIR is not treated as a binary property inferred from a DOI.
Findability, accessibility, interoperability, and reusability are
assessed separately where evidence is available.

2. Dataset and resource inventory

2.1 Vannini et al. (2025) — harmonised geodata collection

-   Resource: Massaciuccoli Lake Basin harmonised geodata collection.
-   Publication: Vannini, G. L., Bove, P., & Coro, G. (2025). Data in
    Brief, 59, 111303.
-   Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.10953582
-   Atlas DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.13912261
-   Contents reported: 148 harmonised geodata layers representing 75
    variables; 109 GeoTIFF rasters and 39 Shapefile vectors; QGIS
    project, metadata workbook and cartographic atlas.
-   Spatial reference / resolution: WGS84 / EPSG:4326; raster resolution
    0.0005 degrees (approximately 50 m); data clipped to the
    Massaciuccoli Lake basin.
-   Temporal frames: remote past (1950–1980), near past (1981–2015),
    present (2016–2024), near future (2025–2050), far future
    (2051–2100); RCP 2.6, 4.5 and 8.5 where available. Not every
    variable is available in every frame/scenario.
-   Access and services: Zenodo; ITINERIS Critical Zone VRE; GeoServer
    WMS/WFS and GeoNetwork metadata services are described by the
    publication.
-   Licence: CC BY 4.0, as stated for the collection.
-   FAIR assessment: Findable and accessible via persistent Zenodo
    identifiers; interoperable through documented formats, CRS, metadata
    and OGC services; reusable under the declared CC BY 4.0 licence,
    subject to attribution and source-level conditions.
-   Status: Verified FAIR resource for the purposes of this catalogue.
    For operational use, retain dataset version and source-layer
    provenance.
-   Important limitation: the article describes a data collection and
    harmonisation methodology; it does not itself provide response
    functions, causal relationships, ecological risk scores, or
    numerical scenario response models.
-   Digital Twin rule: the collection is useful for provenance, variable
    inventory and contextual geospatial consultation. It does not
    override the project rule that quantitative model inputs and outputs
    use the designated project CSV as their sole data source.

2.2 Coro et al. (2024) — modelling inputs, outputs and source code

-   Resource: repository associated with the Ecological Informatics
    study.
-   Repository: Zenodo record 8380273 —
    https://zenodo.org/record/8380273
-   Publication: Ecological Informatics, 81 (2024), 102644.
-   Contents declared by the paper: source code, input and output data,
    ESRI-GRID raster files, tables, images, animations,
    data-manipulation scripts, species list and observation records.
-   Additional environment: ITINERIS Critical Zone Virtual Research
    Environment on D4Science; the paper states that data and scripts are
    continuously updated there and that free registration is required.
-   Licence: the paper describes the code and data as open source, but
    the exact dataset-level licence should be read from the repository
    record and individual files before reuse.
-   FAIR assessment: persistent repository route and detailed content
    description are declared. Exact file inventory, version, metadata
    completeness, licence and download integrity should be checked
    against the live Zenodo record.
-   Status: Partially verified / access declared. The repository
    identifier and declared contents are established from the
    publication; file-level inspection remains a separate step.
-   Digital Twin rule: the repository is documentary and reproducibility
    context only. Its measurements and outputs must not be merged into,
    used to train, calibrate, validate or parameterise the Digital Twin
    quantitative models.

2.3 Pasquetti et al. (2024) — supplementary hydrochemical data

-   Publication: Pasquetti, F., Natali, S., Luppichini, M., Bini, M.,
    Del Seppia, N., Delgado-Huertas, A., & Giannecchini, R. (2024).
    Hydrodynamics and water quality of a highly anthropized wetland: the
    case study of the Massaciuccoli basin (Tuscany, Italy).
    Environmental Science and Pollution Research, 31, 43117–43137.
-   Article DOI: 10.1007/s11356-024-33899-2
-   Data availability statement: the article states that all data are
    available as supplementary material through the article page.
-   Study domain: south-eastern drained sector of the basin, especially
    the Vecchiano and Massaciuccoli sub-catchments, drainage ditches and
    Barra canal; groundwater observations from a small number of
    wells/piezometers; lake sampling limited to one point during the
    June 2021 isotope survey.
-   Temporal coverage: field parameters monitored monthly from October
    2020 to October 2021; groundwater levels extended to 2022 at
    selected points; chemistry on three sampling dates; stable isotopes
    in June 2021.
-   Licence: article is reported as CC BY 4.0. This does not
    automatically establish the licence of every supplementary file.
-   FAIR assessment: article DOI and supplementary-data route are
    identified. Actual supplementary files, persistent dataset
    identifier, formats, metadata, completeness, licence and successful
    download have not all been independently verified.
-   Status: Partially verified / access declared.
-   Important limitation: do not generalise measurements from selected
    ditches, wells and one lake point to the entire lake or the full
    hydrological basin.

2.5 Da Prato & Baneschi (2020) — IGGDATABASE stratigraphic records

-   Publication: Da Prato, S., & Baneschi, I. (2020). The coastal
    wetland systems of northern Tuscany: Massaciuccoli Lake and ex Porta
    Lake. State of knowledge and new opportunities for multidisciplinary
    approach. Acque Sotterranee, DOI 10.7343/as-2020-478.
-   Resource mentioned: IGGDATABASE, described as a QGIS-organised
    collection of stratigraphic logs and sediment cores.
-   Data content relevant to the paper: stratigraphic information used
    to discuss subsurface geology and the need for detailed
    multidisciplinary reconstruction.
-   Access / identifier / licence: not verified for the specific records
    cited in the paper.
-   FAIR assessment: the paper’s reference to a database does not prove
    that the cited records are publicly accessible or reusable.
-   Status: Not verified.
-   Important scope note: the paper is a synthesis and does not provide
    a new basin-wide groundwater model or a new experimental dataset.
    The former Lake Porta is a separate wetland system and must remain
    distinct from Lake Massaciuccoli.

2.8 Ciccolini et al. (2013) — RestoMedPeatland pilot

-   Publication/resource: contribution to the SER Europe Knowledge Base
    on Ecological Restoration in Europe (2010); the supplied document
    describes the RestoMedPeatland project, started in 2011.
-   Study domain: Massaciuccoli Lake floodplain and a 15 ha pilot
    experimental field.
-   Data described: hydrological cycle, surface- and groundwater
    quality, peat oxidation, microbial diversity/functionality, CH4 and
    CO2 emissions, plant nutrient removal, biomass production and energy
    efficiency.
-   Separate repository, persistent identifier and licence: not
    identified in the reviewed material.
-   Status: Not verified as a separate FAIR dataset.
-   Important limitation: pilot-scale observations must not be
    generalised automatically to the entire basin.

4. Required verification actions before operational reuse

1.  Download and inspect the Vannini Zenodo deposit; record version,
    file inventory, licence file and checksums.
2.  Inspect the Coro et al. Zenodo record and capture the exact version,
    file inventory, metadata and dataset-level licences.
3.  Open Pasquetti’s supplementary files and record filenames, formats,
    licences, metadata and any independent persistent identifiers.
4.  Download the HaSCITu archive and inspect CRS, geometry validity,
    attribute schema, version/date, provenance and licence.
5.  Record the exact ITINERIS “Habitat hascitu - Present” resource
    identifier, Zenodo landing page, files and licence; preserve the
    distinction from Viciani’s original GIS.
6.  Investigate IGGDATABASE access and determine whether cited
    stratigraphic records can be identified and reused.
7.  Inspect the original Venturi, Rossetto, Pistocchi, Ciccolini and
    Bertacchi sources for supplementary datasets and data availability
    statements.
8.  Do not promote a resource from “not verified” to “verified FAIR”
    based only on an article citation or catalogue label.

5. Digital Twin data-separation rule

All resources in this catalogue are documentary, contextual, provenance
or external research resources. Their measurements, raster layers,
observations, model outputs and supplementary files MUST NOT be used to
train, calibrate, validate, parameterise or provide inputs to the
Digital Twin’s quantitative models.

The designated project CSV remains the sole quantitative data source for
the Random Forest emulator, VAE-related components, dynamic model, risk
scores and numerical scenario calculations. External sources may be used
only for scientific context, explanation of mechanisms, provenance,
uncertainty and limitations in natural-language RAG responses.
